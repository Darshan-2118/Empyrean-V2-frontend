What the band actually does

Three instruments, one reading you can trust

Empyrean doesn't average your city into a single daily number. It measures the air a few centimetres from your face, tags it to the ground you're standing on, and checks it against thresholds built for your body.

Real-time IoT air mapping

PM1.0, PM2.5, PM10, CO₂, NH₃ and benzene readings stream to the map the moment they're captured, plotted street by street instead of averaged across a district.

MQ135 + PMS5003 · 15s cycle

Automatic GPS tracking

Every reading is geo-tagged the instant it's taken, quietly building your exposure path through the day — no check-ins, no manual logging.

Neo-6M GPS · 1–5m accuracy

Personalized health boundaries

Thirteen profiles across ten conditions and three vulnerability groups set thresholds tuned to your body, so an alert means the air has actually crossed a line that matters to you.
